CORE CASE STUDY
DealLens
AI-Assisted Transaction and Hedging Intelligence
Associate - Derivatives Analytics & AI Solutions Engineer
Assessment formatTake-home case study | 48-hour submission window | Expected working time: approximately 8-12 hours

Candidate-facing assignment

1. Business Context
The Derivatives Risk Solutions team needs a reusable application that converts public transaction agreements into structured, evidence-linked transaction intelligence and uses that information to support preliminary hedging and financing analysis.
You will first develop the model using the Bio-Techne / Merck KGaA transaction. You will then apply the completed model, without transaction-specific hard-coding, to the Organon / Sun Pharma and Uber / Delivery Hero transactions.
Core objectiveDemonstrate that you can connect transaction-document analysis, derivatives and financing judgment, controlled AI extraction, software engineering, and coding-agent workflows in one auditable front-office prototype.

2. What This Case Study Evaluates
Ability to translate contractual provisions into transaction, financing, and hedging implications.
Ability to build modular Python and SQL-based analytics rather than a one-off demonstration.
Ability to design evidence-linked extraction and grounded question answering over complex agreements.
Ability to distinguish source facts, synthetic assumptions, calculations, and analytical conclusions.
Ability to use coding agents efficiently while retaining technical ownership and human control over material judgments.
Ability to validate critical outputs against primary sources and fail closed when evidence is missing or conflicting.
Ability to generalize across transaction types, legal terminology, currencies, and financing structures.
Ability to document controls, lineage, and testing considerations.
3. Source Documents
Use only public information contained in or directly supported by the source documents. Any additional market or financing inputs must be explicitly labeled as synthetic assumptions.

4. Required Workstreams
Workstream 1 - Document Ingestion and Classification
Build a controlled ingestion layer that:
Ingests each PDF and records the source URL, document identity, checksum, filing date, and ingestion timestamp.
Distinguishes the filing summary from the underlying agreement exhibit and other exhibits.
Classifies the transaction structure and applicable document type.
Preserves stable source locators for all extracted evidence.
Detects duplicate, missing, or unreadable pages.
Identifies whether the document is machine-readable or requires OCR.

8. Completion Standard
A successful submission should demonstrate a coherent chain from primary-source evidence to structured transaction data, from transaction data to financing and hedging analysis, and from analytics to a controlled, reproducible front-office workflow.
The final product does not need to be production-ready. It must, however, be runnable, understandable, testable, auditable, and defensible. Material limitations should be explicit, and unsupported outputs should fail safely rather than appear complete.
What matters mostFinancial correctness, evidence quality, modularity, control-minded engineering, real generalization, and demonstrated ownership of agent-assisted work.
